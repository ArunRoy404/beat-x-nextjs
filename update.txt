I wanted to share a quick update on our Backend Development progress. Most parts of both the User and Admin features are now connected and working across the platform. In this update, we are highlighting the modules that have been fully integrated and tested, and we will be sharing the remaining updates very soon:

Admin Login & Security
- We have connected secure admin sign-in with email code verification (OTP) and password reset options

Overview & Analytics
- We have connected the Admin Overview dashboard to display real-time platform statistics
- We have added flexible analytics views allowing you to filter platform metrics across 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, and 1 year

Music & Songs
- We have connected full music and song management with fast search, filtering, and song details
- We have connected Chapter management tools to add, edit, and remove track time-stamps
- We have connected live audio player controls with saved volume settings across pages

Videos & Watch
- We have connected video management supporting Draft, Reviewed, Approved, and Rejected status workflows
- We have set rules so approval and rejection actions apply strictly to Draft status videos
- We have connected the rejection review form with custom reason selection and administrative notes
- We have integrated the video preview player modal with volume memory

Artist Verification & Management
- We have connected identity document checklist validation (ID photo readability, name matching, document expiration, selfie matching)
- We have connected social media profile checks for Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube
- We have added tab review tracking for Overview and Media Assets sections
- We have connected full verification actions: Approve, Reject with custom notes, Request More Info with custom checklists, Suspend, and Reactivate
- We have connected system genre assignment and password-confirmed artist deletion

Users Management
- We have connected user management with fast search, status filters (All, Verified, Unverified), smooth pagination, and user details previews
- We have integrated user deletion and the streamlined new user invitation banner placed beneath the stats cards
